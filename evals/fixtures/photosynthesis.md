# How plants make food

Plants turn light into chemical energy. This happens in the chloroplasts, tiny green parts inside the cells of a leaf. Chlorophyll is the green pigment that captures sunlight.

# What goes in

A plant takes in water through its roots and carbon dioxide from the air through tiny holes called stomata.

# What comes out

Using the energy from light, the plant builds glucose, a sugar it uses as food, and releases oxygen into the air.
