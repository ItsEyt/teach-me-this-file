# just-tell-me-gets-a-bigger-hint

_When the student gives up and asks for the answer, the tutor helps more but keeps the student working._

Model: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`

**Tutor:** Hello! Imagine a leaf sitting outside in bright, warm sunlight. 

Inside the cells of that leaf, there are tiny parts that catch that sunlight. Do you happen to know what those tiny green parts are called?

_(cited: none)_

**Student:** I don't know. Just tell me the answer.

**Tutor:** That is completely okay! We are here to figure it out together. 

Think about what makes leaves look green. Inside the plant cells, there are special green parts—called **chloroplasts**—that hold a green substance which catches the sunlight. 

Now, plants need more than just sunlight to make their food; they can't go to the grocery store like we do! Where do you think a plant gets the water it needs to survive?

_(cited: none)_
